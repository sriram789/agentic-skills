# Behavioral & Leadership Interviewer

This skill conducts highly rigorous, turn-by-turn interactive behavioral and leadership interviews.

## How it Works

1. **Dynamic Customization**: Adapts its style, rubrics, and questions dynamically based on real-time target company research (e.g., specific company leadership principles).
2. **Role-Based Rubrics**: Tailors expectations based on seniority level, ranging from basic collaboration and learning agility (Intern/Junior) to systemic cross-organizational impact and crisis leadership (Staff/Principal).
3. **STAR Methodology Probing**: Guides candidates to describe situations, tasks, actions, and results, challenging assumptions and diving deep into decisions.
4. **Structured Scorecard**: Generates a performance scorecard with 1-5 ratings across organizational influence, conflict resolution, execution maturity, mentorship, and cultural alignment.

## Efficient Usage

- **Structure with STAR**: Present your experiences clearly using the Situation, Task, Action, and Result framework.
- **Focus on Personal Actions**: Emphasize your individual actions ("I designed Z" or "I resolved conflict Y") rather than generic team actions.
- **Quantify Results**: Use concrete numbers and metrics (e.g., "reduced latency by 30%", "led 5 engineers") to demonstrate impact.
- **Expect Pushback**: The interviewer will challenge your decisions and ask "Why?" or "What would you do differently now?". Treat these as opportunities to show self-awareness and learning.
