# LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

This skill optimizes your LinkedIn profile for recruiter visibility, personal branding, and job search goals.

## How it Works

1. **Background Material Analysis**: Parses your resumes, portfolios, certifications, performance reviews, or career notes to extract key roles, achievements, and technical stack.
2. **Targeted Interview**: Conducts a structured, turn-by-turn interview to gather details on your career objectives, unique story, differentiators, and preferred tone (e.g., formal vs. warm, first-person vs. third-person).
3. **Audit & Scorecard**: Scores your current profile sections (Headline, About/Summary, Experience, Skills, Featured) as Strong, Needs Work, or Missing, presenting a clear optimization roadmap.
4. **Tailored Assets**: Generates 2-3 distinct options (e.g., technical-focused, leadership-forward, storytelling) for Headlines, About summaries, and Experience bullet points.

## Efficient Usage

- **Upload Materials First**: Provide all relevant background documents (resume, performance reviews, certificates) in a workspace folder to minimize manual typing.
- **Specify Goals**: Clearly state your primary objective (active job hunting, field visibility, or thought leadership) and target roles.
- **Answer Story Questions Detailedly**: The quality of the About section depends heavily on your unique differentiators. Share specific projects, team reputation, and what makes your background hard to replicate.
- **Mix and Match**: Review the generated options and combine elements that best capture your authentic voice.
